— How This Course Is Built

Not a Device Tutorial.
A Clinical Reasoning Course.

You already know what these lasers do. What you cannot get from a manual or a manufacturer rep is how an expert decides — where to deviate from protocol, when to stop, and what to combine. That is the entire day.

01

Fundamentals Happen Before You Arrive

A required pre-course module covers wavelengths, chromophores, Fitzpatrick considerations, contraindications, and baseline device safety. Nothing on course day is spent on entry-level content — the room starts at an advanced level and stays there.

02

Case-Based, Not Device-Based

Every block opens with a real patient: history, photos, skin type, goals. You commit to a treatment plan out loud before Dr. Saami reveals his. Then he executes and walks through the difference. This is how residents are taught, and it is why the reasoning sticks.

03

Endpoints Called Out Live

Knowing when to stop is the skill that separates competent from excellent, and it cannot be learned from a settings chart. Dr. Saami narrates endpoints as they appear — pinpoint bleeding, tissue contraction, chamois color, erythema quality — on real tissue, in real time.

04

Every Setting Documented

A dedicated clinical scribe records every parameter Dr. Saami uses, on every patient, along with the reasoning behind each deviation. You leave with the actual numbers — not the manufacturer's starting recommendations.

— Technologies Covered

Every Modality, On a Live Patient

Seven treatments across the Sciton platform. Every one demonstrated on a real patient by Dr. Saami — not on a model arm, not on video.

Contour TRL

Full-Face Ablative Resurfacing

Deep full-field ablation. Zonal depth mapping, anesthesia protocol, pass technique, endpoint recognition, and the recovery course you must be prepared to manage.

ProFractional

Scar Revision

Focal treatment by scar morphology. Depth and density selection, series planning, and combination with subcision, TCA CROSS, and intralesional therapy.

Halo Tribrid

Hybrid Full-Face Resurfacing

Balancing ablative and non-ablative wavelengths in one treatment. Density against downtime, coverage strategy, and real-time adjustment.

BBL HEROic

Broadband Light

Filter selection logic rather than filter charts. Corrective versus conditioning strategy, Fitzpatrick IV and above modifications, and HERO parameters.

MOXI

Non-Ablative Fractional

Demonstrated as the second half of a stacked treatment — sequencing, cumulative thermal load, and where it fits in a maintenance protocol.

ClearV

Vascular & Angiomas

Targeted lesion work with an immediate visible endpoint. Cherry angiomas and facial vessels — fast, high-margin, and consistently underused.

ClearSilk

Onychomycosis

A different patient population and a different revenue model. Treatment series structure, realistic expectation setting, and menu integration.

Stacking Strategy

Woven Throughout

What combines safely, in what order, and what does not. Same-day versus staged sequencing, and how to build a treatment plan across a full year.

— What You Leave With

Ready to Use on Monday

Advanced training is only worth what you can still apply six months later. Everything below is designed to survive the drive home.

  • Complete Settings Documentation

    Every parameter used on every patient during the day, captured live by a dedicated clinical scribe — with the reasoning behind each deviation from standard protocol, not just the numbers.

  • Annual Protocol Framework

    A structure for sequencing modalities across a treatment year rather than selling single treatments — the difference between a patient who books once and a patient who books for a decade.

  • Stacking Reference

    What combines, in what order, with what interval — and the combinations Dr. Saami will not perform, with the reasoning behind each.

  • Complication Playbook

    Real cases from Dr. Saami's own practice: what went wrong, what he did, what he would do differently. Including the patients he declines and why.

  • Consultation & Consent Materials

    Patient selection criteria, consent language, pre-treatment preparation, and the post-operative scripting SOM uses for ablative recovery.
